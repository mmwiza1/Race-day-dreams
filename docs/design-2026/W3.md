# W3 — Progress: Running and Overview visual modernisation

Applies the approved expressive direction (`docs/DESIGN_2026.md` §3f–3g) to Progress → Running,
Progress → Overview (hybrid) and the full-screen Training trends page. Presentation only: no
chart, metric, calculation, selection behaviour or chart interaction changed.

## What felt dated

- The measure selector was an outlined segmented row (Distance / Time / Pace) that looked like a
  form control rather than part of the chart.
- Period choices were plain outlined filter chips.
- The chart, its readout and the week's runs sat directly on the page background, with a plain
  "Runs this week" title and a text button for the trends link.
- Overview's split summary was a small, quiet surface with 8 dp dots, and its "Running" and
  "Strength" figure titles were plain text.
- Drill-downs (Run history, Training trends, Running PBs, Body weight, …) were one outlined card
  with stacked link groups under a "More detail" title.
- The Training trends page opened with a plain heading.

## What changed

- **Weekly trend section** (`RunningTrendExplorer.kt`, shared by Progress and Training trends):
  - A `SectionLabel` "Weekly trend" with the trend glyph, in the selected measure's hue (blue
    distance, teal time, violet pace, coral heart rate), captioned with the measure's existing
    heading ("Weekly recorded distance", …).
  - The measure selector, readout and chart sit together in one borderless `Panel`.
  - The measure selector is a borderless tonal connected group: the selected measure fills with
    its own strong chart hue; the others recede on `surfaceContainerHighest`. Tags, selection
    semantics and the measured wrap-to-chips behaviour at 150%+ text or narrow widths are
    unchanged.
  - The headline figure takes the measure's strong hue (the selected bar's colour), so the figure
    and the bar read as one; its label is semi-bold.
  - "Runs this week" / "Runs that week" is a list-glyph section label (with "See all" as its
    trailing action where available) above a borderless panel of the same run rows.
  - "Distance, pace and time trends" is a borderless `ActionGroup` row with a chevron.
- **Period control**: on Progress and Training trends, borderless pill chips; the selected one
  takes the tab's discipline container colour with a checkmark (running primary, strength
  tertiary, overview secondary). The Strength bottom tab and Bouldering keep the previous chips.
- **Running tab**: the running figures are an "At a glance" section (run glyph, blue), matching
  the Strength tab's "At a glance".
- **Overview**: the split summary is one borderless panel holding running and strength at the
  same depth — the same glyph disc (run / dumbbell in the discipline accent), label, figure in
  the discipline colour, and last date — side by side on phones, stacked at 150%+ text. The
  figure sets are labelled sections "Running" (blue) and "Strength" (plum). Data is unchanged.
- **Drill-downs**: glyph-labelled borderless `ActionGroup`s per group — "Running detail" (run,
  blue), "Milestones & body" (body, teal), "Strength detail" (dumbbell, plum) — with the existing
  destination titles and summaries.
- **Training trends page**: a compact running `ScreenHeader` (eyebrow "Weekly running", title
  "Training trends") replaces the plain heading; the period control and Weekly trend section
  follow.

## New internal components (candidates for promotion)

- `ProgressComposition.kt`: `ProgressLinkGroup` and `ProgressDrillDowns` (Progress drill-down
  groups as `ActionGroup`s).
- `PeriodSelector(selected, discipline, select)` overload in `SharedDashboards.kt` (the original
  two-argument form is unchanged).

## Texts and tags

All texts and tags tests use are kept ("Distance this week", "Runs this week/that week",
"See all", "Training trends", "Running PBs", "Body weight", period/measure/kpi tags,
`week-runs`, `trends-range`, `progress-running-summary`, `progress-strength-hero`). The drill-down
group names changed from "Running" / "Strength training" to "Running detail" / "Strength detail"
so section headings do not repeat the tab labels; the "More detail" title is gone (each group now
has its own label). No test referenced these. New tags: `progress-hybrid-summary`,
`progress-drill-downs`.

## Compromises and remaining inconsistencies

- KPI tiles (`KpiGrid`) are shared with the Strength tab and left unchanged.
- The Strength bottom tab still uses the outlined `MoreDetail` card and plain period chips; it
  is outside W3 and can adopt `ProgressDrillDowns`-style groups and the discipline period control
  later.
- The chart now sits inside a panel, so it is 32 dp narrower; its drawing is unchanged.
