# Race Day Dreams 0.12.2-test1

A cohesive Material You refinement, with compact illustrated exercise browsing and consistent filters.

## What's new

- Broad exercise-library categories alongside existing personal, movement, body-area and equipment filters.

## Improvements

- Shared spacing tokens, rounded filter chips with selected checkmarks and accessible touch targets.
- Compact, subtly outlined exercise cards with existing offline illustrations and clear navigation affordances.
- Short body/movement subtitles by default, with complete setup context when searching or refining.
- A real advanced-filter disclosure and short, accessible search labels that stay readable at 200% text.
- Cohesive scrollable tabs for exercise overview, technique, history and PBs.
- Consistent page/card insets and naturally spaced controls across running, gym, statistics, calendar, settings and health.

## Fixes

- Separate wrapped controls with consistent horizontal and vertical gaps, including gym days, equipment and Run Now.
- Give Excluded exercises a clear primary heading while preserving restore and retained history.
- Stack exercise-card content at large text sizes while retaining the illustration and complete labels.

## Known limitations

- Physical Galaxy S24, Pixel, Xiaomi and human TalkBack acceptance remain separate owner checks.
- The forced-narrow emulator retains a partially cropped platform navigation icon; real OEM/rotation checks remain outstanding.
- This is a development/test build. Software verification does not imply physical-device acceptance.

## Privacy and compatibility

No engine, data format, permission, runtime dependency, recording or Health Connect changes.
Navigation icons, labels, order, destinations, branding and launcher icon are preserved.
Existing exercise identities, aliases, exclusions, favourites, history and programme integrations remain.
