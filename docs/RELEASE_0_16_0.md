# Race Day Dreams 0.16.0-test1

This development build updates how running plans build endurance, explain race preparation, and adapt to recent training. The Strength dashboard puts progression and recorded work first.

## What's new

- Race preparation checks explain when your running history or available days limit what a programme can prepare you for.
- Supported running plans include sessions such as 5K or 10K intervals, half-marathon pace repetitions and marathon-pace blocks. Timed jog or walk recoveries sit between repetitions.
- The weekly review can suggest rebuilding from recent recorded running or adding a small amount of easy running. Preview the changes and choose whether to apply them.

## Improvements

- Long runs build from your recent running, available days and recovery spacing. Ordinary sessions use your usual time budget when the app has a pace estimate.
- Recovery weeks and race tapers reduce workload. A race result can guide workout pace when available, and you can use effort when it is missing.
- Strength shows progression, recent sessions and working sets by muscle group.

## Fixes

- A demanding run late in the week no longer causes the following week's long-run distance to silently disappear.
- Accepting a four-week rebuild keeps later training within the rebuilt workload. The old mileage no longer snaps back after week four.
- Workout details show the seconds in timed recoveries. Recovery ends by active time. Its contribution to the total planned distance is an estimate.

## Known limitations

- Readiness checks are a general guide. Race performance and the real-world response to training depend on more than the app can observe.
- Pace targets require suitable recorded results. Short available sessions may stay easy rather than contain full repetitions.
- Outdoor GPS, connected health provider imports and screen-reader use on physical phones require separate device checks. The iPhone development build must be signed before installation.

## Data, privacy and licensing

Training choices and the original programme versions remain on the device. The database and backup format versions are unchanged.
