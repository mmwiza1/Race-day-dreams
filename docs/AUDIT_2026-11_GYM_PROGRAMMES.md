# Gym programme audit — November 2026

**Audited source:** [merged Piece 5B, 7823372c](https://github.com/mmwiza1/Race-day-dreams/commit/7823372c81d3fe09c9485ed6de5ee461d546f593), Android 0.16.0-test1 / 29 and iOS 0.16.0 / 29. All workloads and lifter records in this report are synthetic.

## Executive summary

Race Day Dreams can serve as the primary workout log and programme organiser for a gym-only user. A runner profile is optional. Generated plans and editable templates are credible starting points for novices and general-purpose strength or muscle goals. Manual programming, exact prescriptions, custom exercises, Gym Now session recovery, actual-set history and user-approved revisions are valuable also for experienced lifters.

It is more convincing as a planner and evidence journal than as a fully individualised advanced strength coach. The generator estimates a session budget and balances broad movement roles. It does not know how long a queue will last, where a lifter is truly fatigued, or what specialised competition programming they need. Weekly changes should remain choices. Android has the more complete historical record interface. iOS has shared planning and logging, with fewer routes into PB details and historical editing.

## Independent trajectories

The count of “PB events” below means new high-water marks per measurement bucket. A set may support several buckets. Estimates require qualifying evidence and their own label. Warm-ups and planning-only sets contribute none. The fixture used all standard equipment for these comparisons and excluded back squat and bench press in the first two plans to check selections.

| Scenario | Horizon | Scheduled / logged | Planned phases | Exercise variety | Actual working sets | Record events |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Gym-only beginner, two full-body days, 35 minutes, no effort entered | 12 weeks | 24 / 23 | 3 foundation, 9 development | 7 | 157 | 23 measured, 0 estimated |
| Intermediate half-marathon hybrid, three gym days, 45 minutes, Running First | 18 weeks | 54 / 53 | 2 foundation, 15 development, 1 optional recovery/assessment | 7 | 607 | 106 measured, 5 estimated |
| Experienced lifter, four-day upper/lower muscle programme, 60 minutes, Strength First | 24 weeks | 96 / 95 | 1 foundation, 20 development, 2 intensification, 1 optional recovery/assessment | 11 | 1,963 | 168 measured, 4 estimated |

The hybrid fixture used fixed Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday gym availability, quality running on Thursday and the long run on Sunday. The first 21 days contained six flagged interactions, including Saturday lower-body work before Sunday. No valid move appeared for the first conflict with those hard calendar constraints. The preview preserved both plans and explained the trade-off. This is an example where adjustment of effort, exercise selection or actual availability is more realistic than moving the session.

The separate bench fixture held 80 kg and two sets of six on two recent comparable days, at effort 6/10 against a 4–6 repetition and 5–7 effort prescription. It produced an **optional** 82 kg reference. Missing effort or older evidence produced Hold. Hard, short sets produced Reduce or simplify. The authored RIR target kept its original scale and rest with the same optional reference. Two qualifying sets on each of two plank sessions produced an optional 30-to-35-second change without adding weight. A single session produced no change. These are recommendations derived from fixtures. The load is neither a performed 82 kg lift nor an instruction to increase it.

## Workflow assessment

| Workflow | Actual capability and audit assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Gym-only entry and programme creation | Strength-only entry reaches Gym Now, exercise discovery, manual work and programme generation independently of a race. Typed previews, nine starts/templates, permission-free entry and accepted revision history support ongoing use. Editing a draft and accepting a revision are distinct. |
| Exercise library and instructions | Canonical search and aliases, primary areas, roles, equipment combinations, favourites, recent use and reversible exclusions are supported. Custom exercise measurement can be authored. The exercise browser recognizes user-asserted labels while some analytics require exact canonical definitions. The tests guard fallbacks when equipment is busy or missing. |
| Frequency, movement balance and recovery | Generated upper/lower, full body and push/pull/legs layouts impose role balance and bound set counts by time, experience and priority. Running support includes calf, unilateral and trunk exposure when eligible. A novice can expressly choose a high frequency. Available exercises can be sparse and the generator then warns, trims or rejects the plan. Generation does not forecast an observed biological muscle response. |
| Sets, reps, effort and load | Targets and actual work stay separate, including optional anchor/history/reference, RPE/RIR, timed or distance sets, and rest. Prescribed sets never become achieved volume by opening or accepting the programme. Advanced material is discoverable through disclosures. Generic time assumptions can overrun in a busy gym, particularly with equipment changes or one-sided work. |
| Gym Now, skipped work and substitutions | Assigned days open exact authored sessions. An unplanned log can still be retained. Rest, warm-ups and supersets are opt-in. Persisted actuals, a recoverable paused execution and today's replacement retain traceability. Do not treat an unlogged date as proof of a deliberate skip. The synthetic partial case validated actual-only accounting. This was not a human elapsed-workout test. [Gym Now notes](GYM_NOW.md) |
| Progression and records | Comparable recent, exact-exercise working sets and adequate effort can support a small alternative. Missing effort/loading is unknown. An authored target can suggest a bounded rep, time or distance change at the same measured load. PBs distinguish recorded metrics and estimates. Eligible weighted sets use the bounded Epley estimate for 2–10 reps; it is a calculation, not a lifted maximum. |
| Workload, history and bouldering | The Strength dashboard puts recent accomplishments and progression above tonnage. Primary area shares sum proportionately, secondary involvement is separate, and warm-ups are excluded. Bouldering records climbing sessions, problems, grades, attempts and sends with its own trend view. Climbing grades/attempts do not become gym sets, PBs or a quantified lower-body stress signal. |
| Persistence, edits, imports and deletion | Shared storage keeps frozen programme evidence, actual sets and revision lineage. Android backup and migration exercise associations, active plans and retained histories; iOS Native tests exercise corresponding SQLite archive/restore contracts. The Android app can inspect entered records and history with more detail. Apple Health read support does not automatically import a set-by-set gym history. Watch or health summaries are not safe substitutes for measured loads. |

## Findings and priorities

| ID and severity | Evidence and reproduction | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **G-01 · P2 · iOS lacks the complete PB and history workflow** | On Android, open Strength and choose PB records or historical PB entry. Inspect the equivalent navigation in the iOS host. The shared Strength dashboard and progress charts reach iOS, but there is no dedicated equivalent to the Android PB record and historical-entry routes. The iOS source record page displays recorded sets only and history is simpler. Verified in navigation and source; the iOS gym smoke test only covers basic controls. [Platform parity](PLATFORM_PARITY.md) | **High impact, medium effort.** Share the read/entry components, including measured vs estimated labels, manual date precision, source navigation and edit/delete confirmation. Verify historical backup round-trips and VoiceOver. |
| **G-02 · P2 · Known muscle areas on custom exercises remain unclassified in volume** | Create a custom exercise, assign a body area, and enter a working set. The exercise browser can use the selected metadata, yet the volume engine accepts only an exact canonical match for allocation. The set still contributes to total volume and unclassified counts. This is a conservative implementation limitation with potential confusion, especially for lifters with many customised variants. [Volume policy](../sharedDomain/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/racedaydreams/domain/strength/StrengthVolume.kt) · [Exercise browser coverage](../app/src/androidTest/java/org/racedaydreams/coach/ExerciseBrowseScreenTest.kt) | **Medium impact, medium effort.** Explain the unclassified total in the workload view. Consider separately labelled user-defined area shares only if backups and historical revision handling preserve the chosen metadata. |
| **G-03 · P2 · A detected conflict can have no viable move** | Hold gym days fixed on Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday and run hard Thursday and long Sunday. The 18-week hybrid probe found six near-term conflicts and no alternative for the first. Hard constraints and acceptance held; the limitation is in the options available to a user with a tightly scheduled week. [Recovery analysis](../sharedDomain/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/racedaydreams/domain/recovery/TrainingRecovery.kt) | **Medium impact, medium effort.** Explain the blocking days and show a directly reviewable lighter/lower-body option and why it may help if a date move is impossible. Keep both existing sessions by default. |
| **G-04 · P3 · Budget maths cannot predict busy-gym time** | Generate a 35-minute full-body plan. Estimates honor the configured cap by using fixed preparation, transitions, 4 seconds per rep, 2 seconds per metre or fixed holds, plus rest. Nothing in the model captures walking to another machine, two-sided setup or waiting. [Budget model](../sharedDomain/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/racedaydreams/domain/strength/StrengthWorkoutBudget.kt) | **Medium impact, low initial effort.** Keep the estimate label prominent, disclose assumptions and make optional accessory work easy to identify. Validate any future per-exercise change on real gym sessions. |
| **G-05 · P3 · Climbing contributes no quantified recovery load** | Bouldering history and climbing metrics save and restore separately. Inspect the gym/running recovery inputs, which use gym sessions, run records and optional health outcomes and do not ingest grade/attempt effort. This is appropriate given the incompatible measurements; a climbing-heavy week may still matter to a real athlete. [Climbing analytics](../sharedDomain/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/racedaydreams/domain/climbing/ClimbingAnalytics.kt) | **Medium impact for climbers, medium effort.** Add optional qualitative climbing context before considering a numeric model. Maintain separate evidence and explain why the context may affect an optional decision. |

No P0 or P1 gym defect was reproduced. The record-parity gap is the most consequential for a gym-first iPhone user. Specialised powerlifting/strongman periodisation, provider-imported measured sets, automatic load changes and medical interpretation are outside the verified capabilities.

## Remaining validation

- Long-term physical sessions with equipment queues, repeated heavy lifts, timers with Bluetooth audio and lock screen, multi-day recovery and supporting strength before a half marathon.
- Pausing, app eviction and notification delivery on real phones. Actual Android hardware Gym Now and signed iPhone/Apple Health exercise evidence remain separate from simulators.
- A full one-handed and TalkBack/VoiceOver walkthrough for adding sets, switching substitutes, exercising a superset, deleting a log and confirming the active date or revision. Reviewed 200% visual evidence is selective.
- Large historical data and concurrent active-edit/backups under physical I/O pressure. Database integrity and explicit choice are tested. This audit does not claim a latency target or universally safe loading.
