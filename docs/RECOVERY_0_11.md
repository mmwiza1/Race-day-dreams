# Recovery and training balance — 0.11 development

Recovery & training balance is available from Strength. It is an advisory review of recorded
actuals, not a readiness score or a diagnosis. It does not write actual sets, edit the running
plan or activate a strength revision. Keep my current programme is a no-write choice.

## Original conservative product rules

A recent completed strength workout is demanding when its recorded session effort is at
least 8/10, or at least two working sets have very high recorded effort and those sets comprise
at least half of sets with recorded effort. Very high set effort means recorded perceived effort
(RPE) of at least 9/10, or recorded repetitions left before failure (RIR) of no more than one.
These scales remain separate; missing effort is unknown. Warm-up-only, future-ended and
older-than-14-day strength evidence cannot establish a recovery proposal. Duplicate identical
workouts count once; conflicting duplicate identities are rejected.

Three demanding strength dates in 14 days spanning at least six days make an optional lighter
week available. This threshold is an original, deliberately bounded coaching heuristic, not a
research-mandated fatigue diagnosis. One recent demanding run or strength workout can support
today-only guidance; it does not establish a strength deload. Two fresh source-comparable adverse
sleep/resting-HR/HRV trends can corroborate recorded demanding strength context. Health trends
alone never establish a deload. The existing 4 recent / 14 reference-day evidence gate remains;
future observations and imports are excluded. Health Connect is optional.

## Explicit proposals, existing activation

Preview a lighter week removes one targeted working set per eligible exercise, with a minimum
of one, for the next seven days only. Exercise, load reference, effort, measurement, rest,
superset identity and later weeks remain unchanged. Earlier, completed and started workouts
remain exact. If nothing is eligible, no fabricated change is saved. Actual sets are never
created by a proposal. Today's advice can be followed by logging what was actually performed;
it does not silently override a frozen prescription.

The preview is a typed concrete programme draft using the existing immutable revision,
backup and reviewed-activation repositories. Saving is separate from activation. Evidence and
current programming are reread before saving; activation independently rechecks current pointer,
completed/unfinished execution, eligibility and the exact reviewed draft in a transaction.
The existing programme decision history records accepted changes. Existing earlier-format
programmes retain guidance and manual review; they are not silently converted or overwritten.

## Hybrid schedule context

For Hybrid focus, meaningful lower-body work (including Full Body sessions) is compared with
long, interval, threshold, race-pace, time-trial and race days in the next 20 days. Same-day and
immediately-before placements are conflicts; immediately-after is a softer contextual penalty.
Race context also includes the preceding six days. Priority is explained, never silently
sacrificed. Keeping either session is a valid choice.

Optional move previews offer only unoccupied available dates in the same anchored programme
week and block with a strictly lower scheduling penalty. No available lower-conflict date means
keep or manually review, not dropping a workout. The penalty is an original scheduling heuristic
(same day 4, before 3, after 1, other pre-race days 2), not a physiological load calculation.
Moving retains the exact workout definition and every other dated prescription. Running plans
are never changed by this feature.

## Verification boundary

Pure-domain regression tests cover missing/stale/future/duplicate evidence, RPE/RIR, isolated
health data, protected history, bounded lighter weeks, priority and move proposals. Android
repository/backup, production-screen capture and light/dark/200% interaction tests are defined;
see VERIFICATION.md for observed execution results. No Galaxy S24 acceptance is claimed.
