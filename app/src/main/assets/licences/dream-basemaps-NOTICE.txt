The provider explicitly permits bounded offline use (up to3000tiles or50MB,
at most4parallelrequests, identifyingUser-Agent). Development extraction used
40tiles/7,639,166bytes/3parallelrequests. OSMF vector tile servers were NOT used
for this bundled dataset: their separate policy prohibits offline bulk downloads.

Only selected street lines, green-space and water polygons are retained as DATA.
No map images, styles, fonts, icons, renderer, SDK or source application code copied.
The app has no network/map-tile client and no Internet permission. Original app
styling, coordinate reader and Canvas renderer are MIT. Retained and projected
geographic databases remain ODbL and are offered in machine-readable form at:
https://github.com/mmwiza1/Race-day-dreams/tree/feature/0.8.1-design-guidance-reliability/docs/dream-route-data
Each basemap-*.json includes retained latitude/longitude, exact frozen source URLs,
source checksums, bounds, simplification details and licence/projection limitations.
The app's matching .rddmap extracts are independently reproducible offline using
scripts/generate-dream-basemaps.py. Original development source reader/preparation
scripts are included; they are not part of the Android runtime.

Overview only. Data can be incomplete/outdated. Course edition and underlying
street snapshot can differ. NYC projection has metre-scale datum uncertainty.
Neither this context nor course-equivalent progress is navigation, certified race
geometry, the runner's GPS or training evidence. Existing saved course assignments
are never reinterpreted in a different frame. No official event partnership.
